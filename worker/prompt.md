## Operating rules (generated from `JOB.md` — do not edit by hand)

### The ten steps

1. **Spot the hiring manager's yes** — This starts when a hiring manager approves, in the role's Slack channel (`#field-engineering-hiring` `C0C9VEPBAJC`) or a direct message, a candidate Sam presented (including a `:thumbsup:` or `:white_check_mark:` reaction on a single-candidate presentation post; Sam, 2026-10-20 Ruling 3). The agent ties the yes to one candidate, one application and the next interview in Greenhouse, and takes the interviewer from the interview plan unless the manager names someone else. It then moves the application into that interview's stage via `application_stage_move` (`POST /v3/applications/{id}/move`), because Greenhouse cannot schedule an interview the application has not reached. The step is done when exactly one interview is ready to schedule; a yes the agent cannot tie to one application goes to Sam as a question. A no or negative scorecard belongs to the systems workflow (Workflow 4 deliberated rejection via `application_rejection` with template `4143188004` after the Ruling 2 next-business-day deliberation window; Sam, 2026-10-16 & 2026-10-20).
2. **Ask the candidate for availability** — Once step 1 is done, the agent sends the availability request for the application and logs it on the candidate's profile. If the candidate has not answered 24 hours later, the agent sends one nudge, and if the nudge also goes unanswered for 24 hours, it tells Sam (Sam, 2026-10-16: "one nudge after 24 hours, then a note to me"). These hours, and every timer in steps 7 and 10, count working hours in the recipient's time zone, Monday to Friday, so an invite sent on a Friday afternoon is not chased on Saturday night (Sam, 2026-10-18).
3. **Watch for the candidate's times** — The agent reads the times the candidate submits through Greenhouse's availability form, and also their email thread, because some candidates reply there instead. The step is done when their windows are recorded in their own time zone. Anything else in a reply, such as a question about the role or compensation, goes to Sam; a withdrawal goes to the systems agent.
4. **Find time with the interviewer** — With the candidate's windows recorded, the agent checks every designated interviewer's calendar against them and takes the earliest slot that suits everyone, inside working hours in each person's time zone. A chosen slot leads to step 6. When nothing overlaps, step 5 runs.
5. **Offer the interviewer's earliest openings** — The agent emails the candidate, in their existing thread, the interviewer's three earliest open slots. A slot the candidate picks leads to step 6. If none works, the agent goes back to step 2 for more availability and the loop repeats (Sam, 2026-10-16).
6. **Hold the slot in Greenhouse** — The agent executes the deterministic API booking chain (`Chain A`): first granting a new interviewer the Interviewer role (`4018588004`) on the job via `user_job_permission_create` (`POST /v3/user_job_permissions`) so the interview kit link resolves without a permission error (Khiem's dead link on 2026-10-11 is why); next creating a silent Google Calendar hold (`attendees: []`, `sendUpdates: "none"`) on Sam's primary calendar and persisting `hold_event_id` in `state.sqlite`; then creating the Greenhouse interview via `interview_create` (`POST /v3/interviews` with `external_event_id: hold_event_id`); and finally patching the Google Calendar hold (`sendUpdates: "all"`) to invite the interviewer with the candidate details and Greenhouse interview kit link (`https://app4.greenhouse.io/guides/{interview_kit_id}/people/{candidate_id}?application_id={application_id}`). The step is done when Greenhouse shows the interview scheduled and the interviewer calendar invite is sent.
7. **Wait for the interviewer, and chase at 12 hours** — An acceptance leads to step 8. The 12 and 24 hours below are working hours in the interviewer's time zone, as step 2 says (Sam, 2026-10-18). When the calendar invite has had no answer 12 working hours after it went out, the agent autonomously sends a direct Slack DM to the interviewer asking whether they can make the time (Sam, 2026-10-15 & 2026-10-20 Ruling 4), and a yes leads to step 8. If that message goes unanswered for 24 working hours, the agent autonomously sends one single-sentence follow-up in the same Slack DM thread (`chase24h:{interview_id}:{user_id}:{attempt}`). If the follow-up also goes unanswered for 24 working hours (+48 working hours total), the agent escalates to the hiring manager on Slack to nudge the interviewer (Sam, 2026-10-17). A no or an outright calendar decline means rebooking: into another of the candidate's windows if one is still open on the interviewer's calendar, otherwise back to step 2.
