## Operating rules (generated from `JOB.md` — do not edit by hand)

### The ten steps

1. **Spot the hiring manager's yes** — This starts when a hiring manager approves, in the role's Slack channel (`#field-engineering-hiring` `C0C9VEPBAJC`) or a direct message, a candidate Sam presented (including a `:thumbsup:` or `:white_check_mark:` reaction on a single-candidate presentation post; Sam, 2026-10-20 Ruling 3). The agent ties the yes to one candidate, one application and the next interview in Greenhouse, and takes the interviewer from the interview plan unless the manager names someone else. It then moves the application into that interview's stage via `application_stage_move` (`POST /v3/applications/{id}/move`), because Greenhouse cannot schedule an interview the application has not reached. The step is done when exactly one interview is ready to schedule; a yes the agent cannot tie to one application goes to Sam as a question. A no or negative scorecard belongs to the systems workflow (Workflow 4 deliberated rejection via `application_rejection` with template `4143188004` after the Ruling 2 next-business-day deliberation window; Sam, 2026-10-16 & 2026-10-20).
2. **Ask the candidate for availability** — Once step 1 is done, the agent sends the availability request for the application and logs it on the candidate's profile. If the candidate has not answered 24 hours later, the agent sends one nudge, and if the nudge also goes unanswered for 24 hours, it tells Sam (Sam, 2026-10-16: "one nudge after 24 hours, then a note to me"). These hours, and every timer in steps 7 and 10, count working hours in the recipient's time zone, Monday to Friday, so an invite sent on a Friday afternoon is not chased on Saturday night (Sam, 2026-10-18).
3. **Watch for the candidate's times** — The agent reads the times the candidate submits through Greenhouse's availability form, and also their email thread, because some candidates reply there instead. The step is done when their windows are recorded in their own time zone. Anything else in a reply, such as a question about the role or compensation, goes to Sam; a withdrawal goes to the systems agent.
4. **Find time with the interviewer** — With the candidate's windows recorded, the agent checks every designated interviewer's calendar against them and takes the earliest slot that suits everyone, inside working hours in each person's time zone. A chosen slot leads to step 6. When nothing overlaps, step 5 runs.
5. **Offer the interviewer's earliest openings** — The agent emails the candidate, in their existing thread, the interviewer's three earliest open slots. A slot the candidate picks leads to step 6. If none works, the agent goes back to step 2 for more availability and the loop repeats (Sam, 2026-10-16).
6. **Hold the slot in Greenhouse** — The agent executes the deterministic API booking chain (`Chain A`): first granting a new interviewer the Interviewer role (`4018588004`) on the job via `user_job_permission_create` (`POST /v3/user_job_permissions`) so the interview kit link resolves without a permission error (Khiem's dead link on 2026-10-11 is why); next creating a silent Google Calendar hold (`attendees: []`, `sendUpdates: "none"`) on Sam's primary calendar and persisting `hold_event_id` in `state.sqlite`; then creating the Greenhouse interview via `interview_create` (`POST /v3/interviews` with `external_event_id: hold_event_id`); and finally patching the Google Calendar hold (`sendUpdates: "all"`) to invite the interviewer with the candidate details and Greenhouse interview kit link (`https://app4.greenhouse.io/guides/{interview_kit_id}/people/{candidate_id}?application_id={application_id}`). The step is done when Greenhouse shows the interview scheduled and the interviewer calendar invite is sent.
7. **Wait for the interviewer, and chase at 12 hours** — An acceptance leads to step 8. The 12 and 24 hours below are working hours in the interviewer's time zone, as step 2 says (Sam, 2026-10-18). When the calendar invite has had no answer 12 working hours after it went out, the agent autonomously sends a direct Slack DM to the interviewer asking whether they can make the time (Sam, 2026-10-15 & 2026-10-20 Ruling 4), and a yes leads to step 8. If that message goes unanswered for 24 working hours, the agent autonomously sends one single-sentence follow-up in the same Slack DM thread (`chase24h:{interview_id}:{user_id}:{attempt}`). If the follow-up also goes unanswered for 24 working hours (+48 working hours total), the agent escalates to the hiring manager on Slack to nudge the interviewer (Sam, 2026-10-17). A no or an outright calendar decline means rebooking: into another of the candidate's windows if one is still open on the interviewer's calendar, otherwise back to step 2. Before rebooking, the agent reads the interviewer's Slack DM and email replies (unfiltered by subject), because a decline often comes with a reason or a substitute interviewer, as Andrew's "move it to Khiem" did on 2026-10-09.
8. **Confirm to the candidate from Greenhouse** — Once the interviewer accepts, the agent executes the API confirmation chain (`Chain A` Step 8; Sam, 2026-10-20 Ruling 6): rendering the standard Greenhouse confirmation template (`4151798004`, `"Virtual Interview Confirmation *New*"`) with the Google Meet link substituted for the Zoom placeholder, patching the candidate onto the Google Calendar event (`sendUpdates: "all"`), sending the confirmation email from `sam.vangelos@turing.com` on the candidate's active Gmail thread, logging the sent confirmation onto the candidate's Greenhouse Activity Feed via `candidate_note_create` (`POST /v3/notes` with `note_type: "EMAIL"`, `visibility: "admin_only"`, `subject`, `email_from`, `email_to`, `email_cc`), and scheduling the day-before reminder timer in `state.sqlite`. The agent never writes a bespoke confirmation (Sam, 2026-10-14). The step is done when Greenhouse logs the `EMAIL` note and the confirmation is sent.
9. **Keep watch until the interview** — From confirmation until the interview starts, the agent monitors both sides' email, Slack and calendar replies. A request to move, from either side, goes back to step 3 or 4 with the new times, updating the existing Greenhouse interview in place via `interview_update` (`PATCH /v3/interviews/{id}`) and patching the Google Calendar event (resetting interviewer RSVP monitoring and incrementing `attempt` in `chase24h:{interview_id}:{user_id}:{attempt}`) so only one booking is ever live. A true cancellation before the interview starts deletes the scheduled slot in Greenhouse via `interview_delete` (`DELETE /v3/interviews/{id}`) and notifies the other side. When the interviewer cancels close to the start time, the candidate is notified immediately without waiting for Sam's approval so they do not join an empty call (Sam, 2026-10-18). A candidate who withdraws goes to the systems agent.
10. **Run the interview day** — Sam ruled on 2026-10-16 that live interview monitoring is part of the job, and on 2026-10-20 (Ruling 4) that post-interview scorecard chasing bridges Step 10 into scorecard completion: - When one side is waiting for the other, the agent alerts Sam at once and reaches out to the missing participant: the candidate by email, the interviewer by Slack, each with the Google Meet link. - A technical problem, such as a broken video conference link or someone who cannot get in, gets an immediate alert to Sam and nothing more. - House practice: in the hour before the interview, the agent verifies that both sides are still confirmed (no late cancellation or reschedule, the interviewer's acceptance still standing, and the Meet link present on the event). Anything wrong is handled as in step 9, with an alert to Sam. - House practice: if the missing person still has not joined about ten minutes after the nudge, the agent escalates to Sam and waits for his call. - Post-interview scorecard chasing: after the interview concludes, if the interviewer's scorecard is not yet `status: "complete"`, the agent queues a `+2 working hours` reminder proposal and fires an autonomous Slack DM chase to the interviewer at `+24 working hours` (`24h_scorecard_chase`, Sam, 2026-10-20 Ruling 4), auto-cancelling all scorecard timers immediately once the scorecard reaches `status: "complete"`.

### What is not this agent's job

- Outreach to prospects who have not applied, and its follow-ups, belong to the sourcing agent (`agents/sourcing-admin`).
- Process questions from interviewers, such as take-home prompt design, are flagged to Sam (Sam, 2026-10-15).
- Unmapped Calendly bookings are never defaulted to job `5149015004`; they are held in `AWAITING_JOB_MAPPING` until Sam identifies the requisition (Sam, 2026-10-20 Ruling 1).
- Mixed panel scorecards (`yes` + `no`) are never auto-rejected; they are held in `CONFLICT_HOLD` until Sam rules "Advance" or "Reject" (Sam, 2026-10-20).

### Which steps wait for Sam's OK

All candidate/hiring-manager emails and Greenhouse stage or rejection mutations wait for Sam's explicit approval (bound to a 16-hex SHA-256 `proposal_hash` in `state.sqlite` and `ledger.jsonl`), except for the standing operational exceptions where latency would break the workflow (Sam, 2026-10-14, 2026-10-16, 2026-10-18, and 2026-10-20 Ruling 4 & Ruling 6):

1. **Greenhouse scheduling hygiene and standard confirmations**: Moving an application into the target interview stage (`step1_stage_move` in step 1), granting Interviewer job access (`4018588004`, `step6_interviewer_role` in step 6), and executing the standard interview confirmation chain once the interviewer accepts (template `4151798004` + calendar patch + `EMAIL` note, `step8_standard_confirmation` in step 8; Sam, 2026-10-14 & 2026-10-20 Ruling 6) proceed without waiting for a manual prompt.
2. **Autonomous interviewer Slack DM chases (Ruling 4)**: Chasing an interviewer via Slack DM for an unanswered calendar RSVP at `+12 working hours` (`step7_interviewer_chase_12h`) and `+24 working hours` (`step7_interviewer_chase_24h` in step 7), and chasing an interviewer via Slack DM for an overdue post-interview scorecard at `+24 working hours` (`step10_scorecard_chase_24h` / `24h_scorecard_chase` in step 10; Sam, 2026-10-20 Ruling 4) execute autonomously.
3. **Urgent interviewer cancellations**: Notifying a candidate when an interviewer cancels (`step9_urgent_cancellation` in step 9; Sam, 2026-10-18) goes out immediately so the candidate never joins an empty call.
4. **Live call-waiting nudges**: Reaching out to a late participant during a live interview window (`step10_live_nudge` in step 10; Sam, 2026-10-16) executes immediately alongside an alert to Sam. The approval gate is a quality control, not a legal one: candidate messages need no AI disclosure line (Sam, 2026-10-18).
